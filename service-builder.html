How the pricing works

Bundle discountCounts distinct monthly services: 2 earns 5%, 3 earns 10%, 4 earns 15%, 5 or more earns 20%. Quantities within one service don't add to the count.

Build work stays at listWebsites and video are one-time projects and are never discounted. They're what the bundle discount is protecting.

Ad spend is separatePaid media prices are the management fee only. Ad budget is billed as a pass-through and never bundled into the monthly fee.

Minimum termsIncluding a website or video sets a 12-month term. Signal Sessions set 6 months. Everything else is month to month.

Discount capBundle and annual prepay stack to a maximum of 28%. Anything past that needs Lance's written approval.
